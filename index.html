Good questions

Let’s clear a
few things up.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Start with the task you want to make easier. We’ll help decide whether a simple automation, an AI-powered workflow, or a clearer process is the right fit, and explain the tradeoffs in plain language.

Can you work with my existing website?

That’s part of the initial review. We’ll look at your current platform, what you want to improve, and what access is available before recommending updates or a fresh build.

Will an AI assistant send things without asking?

The examples here use a human review step. For a real project, we agree on permissions, approval rules, and escalation points before anything goes live. AI output can be wrong, so the workflow needs appropriate checks.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope, complexity, and tools involved. We define the work and agree on a quote before building, including any expected third-party costs and the scope of ongoing support.

How do we get started?

Use “Plan your project” to put your goals into a short brief. The builder runs in your browser and doesn’t submit anything. Copy or download your brief, then share it with Rook when a contact channel is available.
